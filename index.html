En plan der lytterA plan that listens

Efter hver tur ser Milo, om du gik i løbeintervallerne. Så rykker du frem, gentager trinnet eller tager et skridt tilbage.

After each run Milo checks whether you walked in the run intervals, and moves you forward, repeats the step or takes a step back.

Milo tells you when to run and walk, and turns your music down instead of stopping it.
